Class 12: If Your Thumbnails Suck, People Aren't Seeing Your Content — Make Them Better with AI

The thumbnail is the most important element of any piece of video or social content—more important than the content itself if nobody clicks to see it. This class teaches you to use AI tools to create thumbnails that actually get clicked.

What This Covers

Class 12 covers what makes thumbnails work (psychology, contrast, faces, text), AI tools for generating and testing thumbnail concepts, how to analyze your competitors' thumbnails with AI, A/B testing thumbnail variations, and creating a repeatable thumbnail creation process using AI that maintains consistent quality.

Why It Matters for Santa Clarita

Santa Clarita business content—whether on YouTube, social media, or email—competes for attention against a constant flood of competing content. Better thumbnails can double or triple your click-through rate without changing a single thing about the underlying content.
