What's In The HVAC Stack

Voice AI Inbound

The agent answers every call in two rings. Natural human-sounding voice (modern ElevenLabs grade, not the robotic 2023 version). Qualifies the homeowner: what system, what's wrong, where's the address, when's the soonest they need it fixed. Books the appointment straight onto your dispatching calendar. The owner gets a notification on their phone. Caller never knows.

Missed-Call-Text-Back Inside 30 Seconds

For the rare call the agent does not catch (system updates, simultaneous calls beyond capacity), an automated SMS fires back inside thirty seconds. "Hi, this is Valley Heating and Air. Sorry we missed you. What's going on with your system? Reply with your zip and we'll get you handled." Recovery rate alone is typically 25 to 40 percent.

Conversation AI Across Every Channel

Same agent brain handles SMS, your website chat widget, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, and Google Business Profile chat. All channels feed into one unified inbox the office staff monitors on a single screen.

Reviews AI Auto-Pilot

Every Google review gets a personalized professional response drafted and posted within minutes. This drives Local Service Ad ranking. Most HVAC shops respond to maybe 30 percent of reviews and take days to do it. Voice AI clients run 100 percent response within an hour.

Workflow Automation Downstream

Once the appointment lands on the calendar, the system fires the confirmation SMS, the day-before reminder, the tech-on-the-way text, the post-service review request, the 90-day follow-up check-in, and the seasonal maintenance plan upsell pitch. None of it requires a human to remember anything.

Questions HVAC Owners Ask

Will my customers know it's AI?
Most do not. Modern voice AI sounds genuinely human. Some shops disclose it in the opening line ("This is our AI assistant, how can I help?"); most do not. Both work. Your call.
What about emergency calls? Won't AI mess those up?
The agent is configured to recognize emergency keywords (no heat, gas smell, flooded) and immediately transfer to whatever phone number you designate. You stay in control of true emergencies. The agent handles the routine intake calls that are 90 percent of your volume.
Does it integrate with ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro?
Yes via webhook bridge. Native HE calendars are the cleanest path if you want to centralize. ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge integrate via custom webhook mapping done once during build.
What if I want to take a call myself?
The agent can transfer mid-call on specific keywords (supervisor, owner, complaint) or on caller request. The transfer routes to whatever number you designate.
How fast does this pay back?
For most HVAC shops doing 30+ calls per week, payback is inside the first month. Single recovered emergency install ticket usually covers the entire year of voice AI cost.
Do I need an A2P 10DLC registration for SMS?
Eventually, yes, for bulk outbound SMS. For the missed-call-text-back response (replying to a call you received), you are generally compliant without it. We help you decide if and when to register based on your actual volume.
