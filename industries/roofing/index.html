Questions Roofing Owners Ask

Will the calculator's home-value numbers hold up?

They anchor to the national Remodeling Cost vs Value report, which tracks how much of a roof replacement is recouped at resale. Asphalt shingle runs around 60%, metal and tile lower. The assumptions show in the result so the homeowner trusts the number instead of dismissing it as a sales gimmick.

Won't homeowners know it's AI on the phone?

Most do not. Modern voice sounds genuinely human. You can disclose it in the opening line or not, both work. The point is the call gets captured instead of lost to voicemail.

What about real roof emergencies, active leaks?

The system recognizes emergency keywords (active leak, ceiling collapse, storm damage) and transfers immediately to whatever number you designate. You stay in control of true emergencies. It handles routine intake and quote requests, which are most of the volume.

I already gave a bunch of quotes. Can it chase those?

Yes, and that's usually where the first month pays for itself. We load your open quotes and the follow-up sequence starts working them the day you go live.

How fast does this pay back?

For most roofing shops, one recovered replacement covers the entire year of cost. The calculator and quote follow-up tend to surface that first job inside the opening weeks.

Does it work with my CRM or do I need a new one?

The cleanest path is the native CRM, which the calculator and call capture feed directly. If you run something else, we bridge it via webhook during the build.
